1. Purpose and deliverables

Design a 3D-printable enclosure and modular mounting system for the Witness camera, a monitoring camera built on the FORIOT ESP32-S3-CAM board. Every part must print on a hobby FDM printer without supports unless noted, and must be fully parametric so the community can change it.

Deliverables, per part:

Format Purpose
Parametric source (OpenSCAD or CadQuery preferred; FreeCAD acceptable) Community edits. Every parameter in this document is a named variable.
STEP (AP214 or AP242) Interchange with other CAD
STL, plus 3MF with print orientation already set Printing
PNG renders: isometric, exploded, and each part in print orientation Docs and README
PARTS.md listing each file, its material, orientation and hardware Assembly

Units are millimeters. Tolerances not stated are ±0.2 mm on the printed part.

Dimensions marked TBV (to be verified) come from vendor listings or clone boards, not from calipers. Before freezing geometry, measure them on a physical board. The Witness team will supply calipered values and photos on request. Every TBV value must be a top-level parameter.

2. System overview

The design has three tiers. Only the board kit changes when a different camera board is supported.

Tier Parts Changes per board?
Board kit sled, front_cover Yes
Universal body rear_shell, sd_door, button_pins No
Feet foot_clamp (+ clamp_screw, clamp_pad, clamp_nut), foot_magnet, foot_template No

The board kit and rear shell meet at a fixed shell interface (section 5.4). The shell and feet meet at the W-Mount (section 6). These two interfaces are the stable contracts. Publish them as separate parametric modules so others can design against them.

3. Reference board: FORIOT ESP32-S3-CAM

Orientation used throughout: hold the board component side toward you, with the antenna end at the top and the two USB-C ports on the bottom edge. “Front” is the component and camera side; “rear” is the side with the microSD slot and header pins.

Camera module (TBV): typical modules are about 8.5 × 8.5 mm with an M8-class lens barrel about 8 mm in diameter. Wide-angle variants (120° and 160°) of the OV3660 fit the same connector. The front window must work with lens fields of view from 66° to 160° (section 5.2).

5. Body

5.1 Overall envelope

The interior is sized to accept the reference board and two planned future boards: the AI-Thinker ESP32-CAM (27 × 40.5 mm) and the Seeed XIAO ESP32S3 Sense (21 × 17.5 mm). Future sleds must fit without changing the rear shell.

Parameter Default Notes
cavity_w × cavity_h 32 × 67 Inside the shell, PCB plane
front_clear 9.0 PCB front face to inside of front cover, covers camera stack
rear_clear 9.5 PCB rear face to inside of rear shell, covers header pins plus SD
wall 2.0 Shell and cover walls
Approx. outside size 36 × 71 × 25 Width × height × depth, before the knuckle

rear_clear may be reduced to 4.5 in a “no headers” variant for users who desolder or never fit the header pins. Expose it as a parameter and ship both STLs.

5.2 Front cover (board kit)

  • Lens window. A round aperture centered on the lens axis (lens_x, lens_y, TBV). The lens front sits no more than lens_recess = 1.0 mm behind the cover’s outer face. Aperture diameter = lens_od + 2 × lens_recess × tan(fov_half) + 0.6, with fov_half = 80° by default so 160° lenses do not vignette. Add a 45° outward chamfer.
  • Camera retention. A square pocket on the inside, sized to the camera module plus fit_slide, holds the module square to the window. A printed spring finger or a 1 mm foam pad behind the module presses it forward. The lens must not touch the window edge.
  • Flex routing. A channel from the camera pocket to the board’s FPC connector, minimum bend radius 1.5 mm, with no sharp edges along the path. The flex must not be creased.
  • Light pipes. Optional 2 mm holes over the power LED and the WS2812 (led_pipe = true). Keep them at least 15 mm from the lens axis and add a 0.8 mm baffle ring so LED light cannot leak into the lens.
  • Button access. Ø2.5 mm holes over RST and BOOT for a paperclip, or captive printed push pins (button_pins part) with a 0.4 mm flexure retention lip.
  • Closure. Mates to the rear shell through the shell interface (5.4).

5.3 Sled (board kit)

  • Holds the PCB by its long edges. Two rails with slots pcb_t + 0.15 wide and 1.2 mm deep. The board slides in from the USB end toward an end stop at the antenna end.
  • A flexing detent tab (0.8 mm thick, 6 mm long) clicks over the USB edge of the PCB to stop it sliding back out.
  • The sled keeps the PCB centered in the cavity so the USB-C ports line up with the rear-shell openings to ±0.3 mm. Register it to the shell with two locating pins (Ø2.0) at fixed positions defined by the shell interface.
  • Underside cutouts leave clear access to the SD slot and to header pins.
  • Thermal: no material within 1 mm of the module shield’s top face; leave an air gap to the vents.

5.4 Shell interface (fixed contract)

This is the boundary between the board kit and the universal rear shell. It must stay fixed so any future board kit fits any rear shell.

Feature Spec
Joint Tongue on the front cover (1.2 wide × 1.5 tall), groove on the rear shell (1.2 + 2 × fit_slide) around the full perimeter
Fasteners 2 × M2 × 8 screws through the front cover into rear-shell bosses, on the long sides, at least 15 mm from the antenna end
Sled locating 2 × Ø2.0 pins on the rear-shell floor; sled holes Ø2.0 + fit_press
Datum Interior cavity center, PCB plane at rear_clear above the rear-shell floor

5.5 Rear shell (universal)

  • USB-C openings. Two openings on the bottom face, each sized to the plug overmold (12.5 × 7.0 mm plus fit_slide), centered on the port positions. Deboss “TTL” and “OTG” beside them. If the two port centers are too close for two full overmold openings, use one combined slot and note it.
  • Cable strain relief. A zip-tie bridge (4 × 2 mm slot pair) on the bottom face beside the USB openings.
  • SD door. A sliding or snap door (sd_door part) over the card slot. The card must be insertable and ejectable (push-push) with the case closed and the door open.
  • Vents. Slots 1.2 mm wide on both long sides, alongside the ESP32 module and away from the lens. Total open area ≥ 60 mm². No direct line of sight from a vent to the camera’s inside face, to keep out stray light.
  • W-Mount knuckle. A two-finger knuckle on the rear face (section 6), centered horizontally and placed 22 mm from the USB end (knuckle_y). The tilt axis is parallel to the short edge, so the camera tilts up and down.
  • Antenna end. Keep the top 15 mm of the shell free of metal and of solid walls thicker than wall.

6. W-Mount interface (fixed contract)

The W-Mount follows the de facto GoPro finger mount, so off-the-shelf GoPro accessories work. Astra must check the final parameters against a genuine GoPro buckle and adjust them until both mate with a firm, wobble-free fit.

Parameter Default Notes
finger_t 3.0 Finger thickness
finger_gap 3.2 Gap between fingers
finger_r 7.5 Outer radius around the pivot
finger_reach 9.0 Pivot center to base surface
bolt_d 5.3 Clearance for M5
Camera side (knuckle2) 2 fingers Total width 9.2
Foot side (knuckle3) 3 fingers Total width 15.4. One outer finger has a captive M5 hex-nut pocket, 8.1 mm across flats × 4.0 deep.
Bolt M5 × 25 thumbscrew or socket-head bolt Printed knob cap m5_knob for socket heads

Print fingers so the layers stack across the finger thickness (finger faces parallel to the bed). This avoids layer splits at the pivot. Fillet the finger roots with r = 1.5.

Publish knuckle2() and knuckle3() as standalone modules in their own file (w_mount.scad or w_mount.py), with a doc comment showing how to attach them to a custom base.

7. Feet

7.1 Screw clamp (foot_clamp)

A C-clamp for shelf edges, robot frames, 2020/3030 extrusion, bench tops and tubes.

Parameter Default Notes
jaw_open 35 Maximum opening; 0 is the minimum
throat 25 Depth from jaw edge to the frame’s back
frame_section 10 × 14 Cross-section of the C frame. Stiff enough in PETG for hand tightening.
Thread Tr14 × 3 trapezoidal, printed fit_thread clearance
Screw length jaw_open + 20
Knob Ø30 knurled, or a 40 mm T-bar (knob_style)
Pad Ø20 swivel pad, snap-fit ball (Ø8) into a socket on the screw tip TPU or PETG face, 1 mm grip ridges
Fixed jaw 20 × 14 face with 1 mm ridges; optional TPU overlay
Knuckle knuckle3 on the back of the frame, axis parallel to the jaw faces by default knuckle_axis = 0° or 90°

Print orientation. Print the C frame lying flat, with the C shape in the XY plane, so bending loads run along the layers. The internal thread would then print sideways, so the frame instead has a hex pocket that accepts a separate printed clamp_nut, printed upright for clean threads and pressed in (fit_press). Provide a variant pocket that accepts a steel M8 nut, with an M8 bolt in place of the printed screw (steel_screw = true). Print the screw upright.

Load check. The clamp must hold a camera (under 60 g) cantilevered 60 mm from the jaw under hand tightening without slipping, and survive 20 tighten and loosen cycles without the thread wearing out.

7.2 Magnet puck (foot_magnet)

A puck that sticks to steel enclosures, machine frames and steel plates. Magnets slide in from the side and are glued in place.

Parameter Default Notes
puck_d × puck_h 40 × 9
magnet_shape disc disc or block
Disc magnets 4 × Ø10 × 3 mm N52 Pockets Ø10.2 × 3.15
Block magnets (alt.) 2 × 20 × 10 × 3 mm N52 Pockets 20.2 × 10.2 × 3.15
skin 0.4 Plastic between the magnet and the contact face. 0 leaves the magnet face flush and exposed.
Pocket entry Each pocket opens to the puck’s outer edge through a slot of the same section, so a magnet slides in radially
Retention A 0.15 mm detent bump at the slot mouth, plus a Ø1.8 glue port from the top face into each pocket for CA or epoxy
Contact face Optional 0.8 mm recess for a 40 mm self-adhesive silicone or TPU anti-slip disc grip_recess
Knuckle knuckle3 on the top face, centered
Pry notch 6 × 2 mm notch on the rim to lever the puck off steel

Print orientation. Print the contact face down on the bed so the skin is smooth and flat. Pocket ceilings are bridges of 10–20 mm, which is acceptable.

Notes for the user guide. Magnet polarity does not matter on thick steel. Alternating N/S between pockets grips thin sheet better. Four Ø10 × 3 N52 discs behind a 0.4 mm skin hold the camera on a vertical steel face with a large safety margin. Keep the puck at least 15 mm from the camera’s antenna end.

7.3 Foot template (foot_template)

A minimal example of a custom foot: a 30 × 30 × 4 base plate with knuckle3, two M3 through holes and a VHB tape recess. Include a short comment block that explains how to attach knuckle3() to any geometry. This is the starting point for community feet such as zip-tie, 2020 T-slot, 1/4”-20 tripod adapter and suction cup.

8. Assembly (for PARTS.md)

  1. Insert the microSD card into the board.
  2. Seat the camera module in the front-cover pocket and route the flex along its channel.
  3. Slide the PCB into the sled until the detent clicks.
  4. Drop the sled onto the rear-shell locating pins. Check the USB-C ports line up with their openings.
  5. Close the front cover onto the rear shell and fit 2 × M2 × 8 screws.
  6. Press the nut into the foot’s knuckle3, join it to the camera’s knuckle2, and fit the M5 bolt.

Hardware list: 2 × M2 × 8 self-tapping screws; 1 × M5 × 25 bolt or thumbscrew; 1 × M5 nut; magnets per 7.2; optional M8 × 60 bolt and nut for the steel clamp variant; optional TPU pads.

9. Acceptance checks

# Check Pass criterion
1 Board fit Board slides in and out by hand. No PCB flex. USB-C plugs seat fully in both ports.
2 Optics No vignetting at 160° FOV, no LED glare in a dark-room capture, lens square to the window within 2°
3 SD access Card inserts and ejects with the case closed and the door open
4 Buttons RST and BOOT can be pressed with the case closed
5 Thermal After 2 hours recording at 22 °C ambient, shell surface stays under 45 °C and the camera keeps running
6 W-Mount Mates with a genuine GoPro buckle and with the printed feet; holds a set tilt with the bolt hand-tight
7 Clamp Load check in 7.1
8 Magnet Holds on a vertical steel plate at the 60 mm cantilever; a 0.4 mm skin survives 50 attach and detach cycles
9 Printability Every part prints without supports in PETG at 0.2 mm, in the 3MF orientation
10 Wi-Fi RSSI at 5 m is no worse than 6 dB below the bare board, measured with the magnet puck on a steel plate

10. Open questions for Astra

  1. Confirm the TBV dimensions in section 3 from a physical board, especially the lens position, USB-C centers, button and LED positions, and SD insertion edge.
  2. If a camera flex is too short to put the lens where the front cover needs it, propose the lens position and we will standardize on it.
  3. Advise whether OpenSCAD or CadQuery gives the community the better parametric source for these shapes.
